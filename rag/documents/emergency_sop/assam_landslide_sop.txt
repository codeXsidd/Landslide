ASSAM STATE DISASTER MANAGEMENT AUTHORITY (ASDMA)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) FOR LANDSLIDE RESPONSE

SECTION 1: ROAD BLOCKAGE AND ISOLATION
1.1 Immediate Assessment:
Upon receiving a verified report of a landslide blocking a primary arterial road (e.g., Road B Corridor):
- The District Emergency Operation Centre (DEOC) must immediately dispatch a Rapid Damage Assessment (RDA) team.
- Aerial or satellite verification should be requested if on-ground access is deemed hazardous.

1.2 Village Isolation Protocol:
If a road blockage results in the isolation of a village (population > 500):
- Priority 1: Establish communication via satellite phones or HAM radio if mobile networks fail.
- Priority 2: Pre-position heavy earth-moving equipment (excavators, bulldozers) at the nearest accessible junction.
- Priority 3: Assess alternate routes for Light Motor Vehicles (LMVs) to ensure medical supplies can reach the isolated population.

1.3 Medical Facility Degradation:
If access to the nearest District Hospital or Community Health Centre (CHC) is degraded:
- Deploy Mobile Medical Units (MMUs) to the safe side of the blockage.
- Identify the nearest functional Helipad for emergency medical evacuation.

SECTION 2: EARLY WARNING NOTIFICATIONS
2.1 When the Landslide Risk Score exceeds 80% (HIGH) and Confidence is HIGH (>80%):
- Issue an SMS/Cell Broadcast warning to all residents within a 5km radius.
- Traffic police must pre-emptively close highly susceptible road corridors to heavy vehicles.

SECTION 3: EVIDENCE CONTRADICTIONS
3.1 In cases where satellite data and ground reports contradict each other:
- Ground reports verified by Computer Vision (CV) or local authorities take precedence over stale satellite imagery.
- Do not lift evacuation orders until a physical inspection is complete.
